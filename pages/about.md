## About FPJ

Fernando Poe Jr. was born Ronald Allan Kelley Poe in Manila on August 20, 1939, the son of actor Fernando Poe Sr. He entered the film industry as a stuntman after his father's death and made his screen debut in 1955 in *Anak ni Palaris*.

Over the next five decades he appeared in around 300 films, and became known simply as FPJ, or "Da King" of Philippine cinema. He also directed, often under the name Ronwaldo Reyes.

He won the FAMAS Award for Best Actor five times and entered the FAMAS Hall of Fame in 1988. He ran for president in 2004 and died in Quezon City on December 14 of that year. He was later named a National Artist of the Philippines for Film.
